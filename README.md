## About this entry

An apology for a mishearing. The joke is the swap — "bring snacks"
heard as "bring facts" — which is funny because the two words are
nothing alike, yet the mistake is entirely believable in a loud
room. The payoff is the result: the speaker arrived empty-handed
and overprepared, which is the exact opposite of what the party
needed and the exact opposite of what the speaker intended. The
apology does not grovel; it reports the damage with the confidence
of someone who brought a lecture to a potluck and only noticed at
the door. The program holds the line, checks it against the one
hundred and forty character cap, and writes it to /output/entry.txt
exactly as the bounty requires.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the one line to `/output/entry.txt`.
